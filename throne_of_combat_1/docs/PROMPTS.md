# AI Graphics & Animation Prompts - Throne of Combat 1

## PART 1: 3D CHARACTER MODELS (Use [Meshy.ai](https://www.meshy.ai/) or [Luma AI](https://lumalabs.ai/genie))

1. **Knight (Heavy Melee)**
   - **Prompt:** Full body 3D model of a dark fantasy knight, black and crimson ornate plate armor, glowing red eyes behind a visor, carrying a massive black steel sword and a jagged shield with a dragon emblem. High-detail, cinematic lighting, Unreal Engine 5 render style.
2. **Assassin (Agile)**
   - **Prompt:** Full body 3D model of a futuristic ninja, blue and teal glowing mesh attire, dual cyan energy daggers, masked face with tactical HUD, sleek and aerodynamic silhouette. Anime-style aesthetics, vibrant neon accents.
3. **Mage (Caster)**
   - **Prompt:** Full body 3D model of a powerful wizard, flowing deep purple robes with gold embroidery, three glowing arcane orbs orbiting the head, white long beard, holding a crystal staff. Dark fantasy, magical particles.
4. **Bestial Warrior (Bruiser)**
   - **Prompt:** Full body 3D model of a humanoid beast, muscular build, lion-like features, wearing primitive bone armor and beast-hides, massive iron claws attached to hands. Brutal and aggressive pose, realistic fur textures.
